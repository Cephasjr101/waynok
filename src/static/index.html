Support

How do I post a load?

Open the Load board and tap "Post a load". Fill in origin, destination, weight and pickup time — you'll be matched with drivers instantly.

When does the driver get paid?

When you confirm delivery, the payment is released to the driver's withdrawable balance. Drivers withdraw to MoMo or bank.

How do driver earnings work?

Drivers keep 85% of the quoted price. The remaining 15% covers platform operations and payment processing.

Contact us

Email support@waynok.net and we will reply within one business day.

File an insurance claim

Accident or cargo loss? Both parties submit evidence and Waynok compensates up to GHS 1,000,000. Reviewed within 72 hours (3 business days).

Terms of service

1. The service

Waynok connects shippers with independent truck drivers across Ghana. Waynok is a marketplace, not a carrier; the contract of carriage is between shipper and driver.

2. Accounts

You must provide accurate information. One account per person or company. You are responsible for activity under your account.

3. Payments

Quoted prices are system-generated from distance, weight and equipment. Shippers pay on delivery confirmation. Drivers receive 85% of the quoted price.

4. Conduct

No fraudulent loads, no off-platform payment solicitation, no abuse of other users. Violations lead to suspension.

5. Insurance & compensation

Waynok provides accident compensation coverage of up to GHS 1,000,000 per incident when both the shipper and the carrier provide sufficient evidence (photos, police report where applicable, and delivery documentation). Claims are reviewed within 72 hours (3 business days) and approved payouts are paid within the same window.

6. Liability

Waynok facilitates matching and payment but is not liable for loss or damage to cargo. Drivers are responsible for the goods in their custody.

Privacy policy

What we collect

  • Account details you give us: name, email or phone, payout details.
  • Load details you post and location you share while online.
  • Usage data that helps us keep the service fast and secure.

How we use it

  • To match loads with drivers and run payments.
  • To show your live position only while you are online, and only on the live map.
  • To send service emails you can switch off in Settings.

What we never do

We never sell your data. We never share your payout details with other users.

Your control

Email privacy@waynok.net to export or delete your account data.

Refund policy

Shippers

If a load is cancelled before pickup, your payment is refunded in full within 72 hours (3 business days). If delivery fails after pickup, contact support with your load ID for a full review.

Drivers

Earnings already released to your balance are yours. Withdrawals are processed within 48 hours to your saved MoMo or bank account.

Disputes

Email support@waynok.net with your load ID. We resolve most disputes within 72 hours.
