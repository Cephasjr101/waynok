Support

How do I post a load?
Sign in as a Shipper, go to the Loads tab, fill in pickup, dropoff, weight and price, then hit "Post load". It appears on the board instantly.
How does the AI dispatcher work?
Open the AI Dispatcher tab and type naturally — e.g. "find me a load from Accra to Kumasi". The agent searches the board, computes distances, and estimates prices for you.
How do I list my truck?
Sign in as a Carrier, open "List your truck" from the menu, and submit your truck details. Available trucks appear on the Track tab immediately.
Who do I contact for payment issues?
Email dispatch@waynok.com. Payouts are processed weekly every Friday.

Still stuck? Reach us at dispatch@waynok.com — average response under 4 hours.

Safety on the road

✚Rest every 4 hours. Fatigue is the #1 cause of haulage accidents. Plan breaks into every route.
✚Verify loads before pickup. Confirm weight and paperwork match the board listing before loading.
✚Share your live location. Keep tracking on so dispatch always knows where you are.
✚Never share your login. Waynok staff will never ask for your password or token.
✚Emergency? Call Ghana emergency services on 112, then notify dispatch.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. We collect the minimum data needed to run the load board.

◈What we collect. Account details (name, email, role), your listings (loads or trucks, including cities and coordinates you enter), and activity such as logins and matches.
◈How we use it. To match loads with nearby drivers, display your listings to other users, keep you safe, prevent fraud, and improve the service. We do not sell personal data.
◈Storage. Preferences and activity on your device are stored in your browser's local storage. Server data is retained while your account is active and deleted on request.
◈Security. Passwords are hashed and API traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
