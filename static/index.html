Support

How do I post a load?
Go to the Loads tab, fill in pickup, dropoff, weight and price, then hit "Post load". It appears on the board instantly.
How does the AI dispatcher work?
Open the AI Dispatcher tab and type naturally — e.g. "find me a load from Accra to Kumasi under 20k lbs". The agent searches the board, computes distances, and estimates prices for you.
How do I track a truck?
Live GPS tracking appears on the Map tab once drivers start pinging their location from the mobile app.
Who do I contact for payment issues?
Email dispatch@waynok.com or call the operations line. Payouts are processed weekly every Friday.

Still stuck? Reach us at dispatch@waynok.com — average response under 4 hours.

Safety on the road

✚Rest every 4 hours. Fatigue is the #1 cause of haulage accidents. Plan breaks into every route.
✚Verify loads before pickup. Confirm weight and paperwork match the board listing before loading.
✚Share your live location. Keep GPS pinging on so dispatch always knows where you are.
✚Never share your login. Waynok staff will never ask for your password or token.
✚Report unsafe conditions. Bad roads, theft hotspots, or unsafe pickup sites — flag them via Support.
✚Emergency? Call Ghana emergency services on 112, then notify dispatch.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. We collect the minimum data needed to run the load board.

◈What we collect. Account details (name, email, role), your listings (loads or trucks, including cities and coordinates you enter), location pings you explicitly share while on a trip, and activity such as logins and matches.
◈How we use it. To match loads with nearby drivers, display your listings to other users, keep you safe, prevent fraud, and improve the service. We do not sell personal data.
◈Sharing. Your listing details are visible to matched parties (e.g., a shipper sees the assigned driver's name, plate, and live position while on trip). We may share data with service providers that power the platform (hosting, maps, authentication) or when legally required.
◈Location. Precise GPS is used only when you list a truck or go on trip, and only to show customers your position. You can deny browser geolocation; the app falls back to your city.
◈Storage. Preferences and activity on your device are stored in your browser's local storage and never leave your device. Server data is retained while your account is active and deleted on request.
◈Security. Passwords are hashed and API traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to production data is restricted.
◈Your rights. You may request a copy, correction, or deletion of your data at any time by emailing dispatch@waynok.com.

Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026.

1Platform commission. If a matched load is cancelled by the carrier before pickup, any Waynok commission charged on that load is automatically refunded to the shipper within 5 business days.
2Cancelled loads. Loads cancelled before a driver is assigned carry no charge. Loads cancelled after connection but before pickup are reviewed; repeated cancellations may limit account features.
3Carrier payouts. Payouts for completed deliveries are processed every Friday. A delivery is considered complete when the load is marked delivered in the app. Incorrect payouts are corrected in the next cycle.
4Disputes. Raise any refund request within 14 days of the charge by emailing dispatch@waynok.com with the load ID. We aim to resolve disputes within 7 business days.
5Non-refundable. Completed hauls, no-show fees where the shipper failed to provide the cargo as listed, and chargebacks made in bad faith are not refundable.
We store preferences in your browser and load privacy-friendly analytics only if you accept. Read our Privacy Policy.
